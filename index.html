Frequently Asked Questions

Does Wordiz work offline?

Yes. Your vocabulary library is stored locally on your device, so capturing, browsing, and practicing words all work without an internet connection. Some AI-assisted features (like generated exercises or speaking feedback) require a connection.

Is my data private?

Your vocabulary and study data are stored locally on your device by default. If you have questions about a specific feature's data handling, email us and we'll explain exactly what's stored and where.

How do I report a bug or request a feature?

Email yurik1776@gmail.com with a description of the issue (screenshots help!) and your device model and iOS/Android version.

How do I delete my data?

You can delete individual words or your entire library from within the app's settings. If you need help removing data completely, contact us at the email above.
